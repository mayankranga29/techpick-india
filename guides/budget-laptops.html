BUDGET PLANNING

Set a laptop budget that fits your real needs

A useful laptop budget starts with your workload and how long you expect to keep the device. Avoid paying extra for features you will not use, but do not overlook basics that are difficult or impossible to upgrade later.

Start with your main tasks

List your most common activities and the software you need. A laptop for documents, classes and browsing has different requirements from one used for software development, 3D work or gaming.

Keep room for the full cost

Consider whether you also need a laptop bag, mouse, external storage, a monitor, software or an extended warranty. Include these costs when deciding what you can comfortably spend.

Prioritise hard-to-change features

Pay close attention to display quality, build, keyboard, battery and whether memory is upgradeable. These are important to check before purchase because some cannot be changed later.

Compare exact configurations

The same laptop family can be sold with different processors, displays, memory and storage. Compare full model numbers and verify the seller's listing before placing an order.

Do a final value check

Read independent reviews, check warranty and return conditions, and compare the final price from reliable sellers. A discount is useful only if the configuration and support meet your needs.
